## TexMacgic Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 23, 2026**

TexMacgic is designed as a local, native macOS LaTeX editor. The Mac App Store version of TexMacgic does not collect personal data from users.

### Data collection

TexMacgic does not:

- require an account;
- include advertising or third-party analytics;
- collect or transmit document contents, filenames, project paths, or usage activity;
- sell or share personal information.

### Local documents and compilation

Documents remain in locations the user explicitly selects on their Mac. LaTeX compilation uses the compatible TeX distribution installed and enabled by the user on that Mac. TexMacgic does not upload documents for compilation.

The initial Mac App Store release does not enable external coding-agent integrations or Apple Intelligence features.

### Apple services

Apple may process App Store downloads, purchases, crash information, or other service data under Apple's own policies and user settings. TexMacgic does not receive personal data from Apple for analytics or advertising.

### Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how TexMacgic handles data, this policy and the app's App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

### Contact

For privacy questions or support, please [open a TexMacgic support issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Calvin1127XD/Calvin1127XD.github.io/issues/new?title=TexMacgic%20Privacy%3A%20&labels=texmacgic). Do not include confidential documents or personal information in a public issue.
